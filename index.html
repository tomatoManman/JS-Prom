Welcome to the PSHS-BRC Batch 2027-2028 Promenade!

The Junior–Senior Promenade, titled Rêveuse: The Night the Flowers Remembered, will be held on February 20, 2026. It is an event organized by the Batch 2027 and Batch 2028 Councils, under the guidance of Mr. Joel R. Reginales, and with the support of the Grade 10 and Grade 11 advisers.

"Whimsical / Ethereal / Gardencore

Theme

Rêveuse seeks to blend elegance and creativity in an atmosphere that celebrates youth, growth, and unity. This Promenade also serves as a culminating social activity for scholars of Grades 10 and 11. It provides an avenue for students to demonstrate proper social etiquette in a formal setting while commemorating a meaningful milestone in their Pisay-Bikol journey.

This event is complete with traditional features such as:

  • The Cotillion
  • Bachelorette Dance
  • Banquet
  • Grand Ball
  • Intermission numbers

Around 180 students—comprising 90 scholars from Batch 2027 and 90 from Batch 2028—are expected to attend, alongside class advisers, subject teachers, and other faculty members who will serve as guests and chaperones.
